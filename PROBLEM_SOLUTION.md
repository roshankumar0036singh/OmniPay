# OmniPay: Problem, Solution & Features

## 🔴 The Problem: E-Commerce is Fragmented
Despite the internet connecting the world, **shopping remains locally siloed.**
A user in the US cannot easily buy from *Amazon Japan*, *Taobao (China)*, or *Mercari (Germany)*.

### The 3 Great Barriers:
1.  **Language Wall:** Product descriptions, sizing charts, and checkout forms are unintelligible. Google Translate breaks layouts and misses context (e.g., technical specs).
2.  **Payment Friction:** Foreign sites often reject international credit cards or require local payment methods (e.g., Konbini in Japan, Alipay in China).
3.  **Logistics Black Hole:** Most sellers do not ship internationally, or shipping costs are opaque and exorbitant.

---

## 🟢 The Solution: OmniPay
**"Shop the world like a local."**
OmniPay is a browser extension and proxy service that flattens the world of e-commerce into a single, unified experience.

### How it works:
*   **For the User:** It feels like browsing a local site. Everything is in English, prices are in USD, and checkout happens once.
*   **Under the Hood:** OmniPay acts as a "Universal Proxy". We translate the content, process the payment locally, and handle the cross-border logistics.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🛒 Universal Checkout (The Core)
*   **Feature:** A single "Add to Global Cart" button that works on **any** supported site (Amazon JP, Rakuten, etc.).
*   **Highlight:** Users pay *OmniPay* in their local currency. We handle the complex foreign transaction instantly.

### 2. 🌐 global_search (Discovery)
*   **Feature:** Search in English -> Find results globally.
*   **Tech:** "Sony Headphones" is auto-translated to "Sony ヘッドホン" (JP) to find local deals that are invisible to English speakers.
*   **Benefit:** Unlock "Arbitrage" opportunities (e.g., buying cameras cheaper from Japan).

### 3. 🧠 Contextual Translation (Lingo.dev)
*   **Feature:** AI-powered translation that understands e-commerce context.
*   **Highlight:**
    *   **Fashion:** Translates "Waist: 80cm" to "US Size 32".
    *   **Tech:** Translates specific model numbers and specs accurately.
    *   **Reviews:** Summarizes native sentiment ("Locals say this runs small") instead of broken literal translations.

### 4. ⚖️ The Ethical Scanner (Transparency)
*   **Feature:** Scans product descriptions for "Origin" and "Material" keywords.
*   **Highlight:** Exposes supply chain truths (e.g., "Made in X", "Synthetic") that are often hidden in foreign text.

### 5. 🛡️ Support Proxy
*   **Feature:** If a seller sends an email in Japanese (e.g., "Shipping Delayed"), OmniPay intercepts it, translates it to English, and forwards it to the user.
